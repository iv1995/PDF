12.7.9Non-interactive forms 
 
Unlike interactive forms, non-interactive forms do not use widget annotations but are represented with 
page content. Non-interactive forms are defined by the PrintField attribute (14.8.5.6, "PrintField 
Attributes") for repurposing and accessibility purposes.
