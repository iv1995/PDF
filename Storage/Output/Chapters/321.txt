12.4.3Articles 
 
Some types of documents may contain sequences of content items that are logically connected but not 
physically sequential. 
EXAMPLE 1 A news story may begin on the first page of a newsletter and run over onto one or more nonconsecutive 
interior pages. 
PDF 1.1
To represent such sequences of physically discontiguous but logically related items, a PDF document 
may define one or more articles (). The sequential flow of an article shall be defined by an article 
thread; the individual content items that make up the article are called beads on the thread. Interactive 
PDF processors may provide navigation facilities to allow the user to follow a thread from one bead to 
the next.
