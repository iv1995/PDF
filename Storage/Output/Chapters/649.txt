Parent-child relationships between standard structure elements 
#(Normative) 
The table in this Annex sets out the acceptable children of the standard structure elements defined in 
#PDF #2.0. 
A matrix representation of this information is attached to this document as “ISO32000-
2_AnnexL_matrix-version#.pd#f”. 
The structure elements in the leftmost columns are acceptable child elements of the structure elements 
in the Parents column. Values in the occurrence column indicate the valid usage of standard structure 
types relative to other standard structure types as follows: 
1 shall occur as a child element exactly once 
0..n may be a child element with one or several occurrences, but is not required to be present 
1..n shall be present as a child element with one or several occurrences 
0..1 may occur, but not more than once 
∆ may contain any amount of content or no content 
If not explicitly allowed by this table, a child element shall not occur.
