"Figure 109: 3D Angular Dimension Measurement" shows an angular measurement markup consisting 
of two anchor points, one located on each of the two linear entities whose angle is being measured. 
Connected to each anchor point is an extension line that is collinear with the edge it measures. A 
labelled arc, with an arrowhead at each end, connects the two extension lines, making it clear which 
angle is being measured.
