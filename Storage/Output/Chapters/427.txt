12.8.2.2.2Validating signatures that use the DocMDP transform method 
 
 DocMDP 
To validate a signature that uses thetransform method, a ##PDF processor first shall verify the 
#byte range digest. Next, it shall verify that any modifications that have been made to the document are 
permitted by the transform parameters. 
 #ByteRange 
Once the #byte range digest is validated, the portion of the document specified by theentry 
in the signature dictionary (see "#Table 255: Entries in a signature dictionary") is known to correspond 
to the state of the document at the time of signing. Therefore, ##PDF processors may compare the signed 
and current versions of the document to see whether there have been modifications to any #objects that 
are not permitted by the transform parameters.
