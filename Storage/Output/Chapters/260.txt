error if a CMap is encountered that is not predefined for that PDF version. If character codes are 
encountered that were added in a higher-numbered supplement than the one corresponding to the 
supported PDF version, no characters are displayed for those codes; see 9.7.6.3, "Handling undefined 
characters".
